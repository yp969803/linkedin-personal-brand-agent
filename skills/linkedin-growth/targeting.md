# Who and what to engage

## Authors (read the headline/title live from the page)
Comment on posts by people who fit "Who I want as inbound" in the profile. Founder/exec headlines are the priority: CEO, CTO, founder, co-founder, cofounder, founding engineer, founding team, or equivalent ("Chief Executive", "Chief Technology"). Also fine for comments: engineers and researchers on the profile's topics, open-source maintainers, and engineering or research leaders hiring for the profile's areas. These are signals, not a list of people. Skip generic recruiters, coaches and agency "growth" accounts.
Also skip: people the profile lists as competitors or excluded, the user's own employer's staff if the profile says so, and anyone already in the log (`STATE seen`).

## Posts
Engage only if all are true:
- On the profile's topics (or close to them).
- Posted in the last 3 days.
- Has original text from the author (not a bare reshare, ad, or giveaway).
- Technical or substantive (engineering, product, building a company). Skip motivational and generic advice posts.
- Not politics, religion, culture war, outrage, jokes/memes, layoffs or hiring drama, personal tragedy, or engagement bait.
- You can add one specific, true thing.

If unsure, skip.

## Connection requests
Connect list: CEO, CTO, founder, co-founder, founding engineer, and open-source maintainers of projects on the profile's topics (Kubernetes, Prometheus, AI infra and similar), read from the headline or profile. Researchers, engineers and hiring leaders get comments only. Send only to authors who passed the checks above (or commenters on the user's posts who are on the connect list), who are not already connected or pending. No sales note. Optional one line: "Liked your post on <topic>. Would like to follow your work."
